7.3 Object Trajectory in the World Frame

The ArUco marker positions are transformed from the camera frame into the calibrated world frame and levelled using the gravity direction. The evaluation separates the independently measured rail geometry, a straight line fitted to the filtered ArUco samples, and the individual samples around that line.

The physical rail is 38.7 centimetres long and 3.8 centimetres high, and was set horizontal with a spirit level. The independently measured route comprises a 25.5 centimetre approach, a 3.5 centimetre lift and a 37.5 centimetre slide. The rail endpoints and its direction within the horizontal plane have not been independently registered in the world frame. Validation therefore covers relative height and departure from horizontal, without claiming absolute three-dimensional position or orientation accuracy.

A straight line is fitted to the slide samples by minimising their summed squared perpendicular distances. The line estimates the observed trajectory and reduces the influence of frame-to-frame tracking noise. Its direction is compared with the horizontal rail setup. In both recordings, the fitted line lies within 0.1 degrees of horizontal in the calibrated frame. This comparison includes uncertainty in the gravity direction and does not measure the cube's orientation.

The difference between the tracked rail and desk height modes is 4.0 centimetres in the rail recording and 4.4 centimetres in the handover recording. These relative height estimates exceed the physical rail height by 0.2 and 0.6 centimetres. The comparison assumes that the marker retains the same height relative to the cube base on the desk and rail. The selected samples span 40.9 and 42.4 centimetres along their fitted lines. These extents describe the tracked intervals; their endpoints are not matched to the measured rail or route endpoints, so no length error is assigned.

Tracking scatter is reported separately as perpendicular deviation of individual filtered ArUco samples from the fitted line. In the rail recording, the median, 95th percentile and maximum are 1.0, 2.4 and 3.1 centimetres. The median absolute vertical component is 0.3 centimetres and the median horizontal component is 0.8 centimetres. In the handover recording, the perpendicular deviations are 0.3, 1.6 and 3.4 centimetres, respectively. This scatter includes tracking noise remaining after filtering and physical departures of the cube from a straight path.
