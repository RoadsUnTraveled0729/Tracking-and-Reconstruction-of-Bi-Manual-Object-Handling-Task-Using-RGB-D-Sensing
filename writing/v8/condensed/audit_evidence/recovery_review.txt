FINDING R5. MEDIUM: Appendix E.2 angular separation is not a general guarantee of perspective disambiguation (additional bounded review requested by parent).
Location: condensed/scripts/build_appendix.py:245, paragraph beginning 'How far apart the two lobes sit...'.
Quote: 'A marker whose projection is nearly an affine copy of the printed square gives almost no perspective cue, and the lobes collapse toward each other.'
Exact counterexample: under orthographic projection, rotate planar points (X,Y,0) about y by +theta or -theta. Both project to (cos(theta)X,Y), while normals differ by 2theta. At theta=30 degrees, the affine images are identical but normals differ by60 degrees, exceeding the40-degree rule. The same indistinguishability is approached in distant small-target perspective imaging. Lobe angular separation and reprojection distinguishability are different quantities.
Correction: describe 40 degrees as an implementation heuristic, not a general projective-conditioning result. Establish branch distinguishability using reprojection residual separation relative to corner uncertainty; retain physical/temporal priors where ambiguity persists. This is a self-contained geometry derivation, not a citation-dependent recollection.
